04

Charts

Read the relationship first, then pick the form. One chart per slide, one accent per series, labels on the chart instead of a legend, rounded caps, thin gridlines, no library defaults.

Soft area, trend over time One series moving through time.
Donut, part to whole Key number sits in the hole.
Horizontal bars, ranking Rounded ends, value at the tip.
Ring gauge, progress A single percentage or target.
Paired bars, two metrics Same category, two measures.
Funnel, conversion How a total splits down a path.
Gantt, phases Campaign or project schedule.
Bubble, correlation Two variables, size as weight.
05

Device visuals

One or two phone frames at a slight angle, soft diffuse shadow, frosted cards floating out beside the screen, a blurred pastel orb behind, and a large low-contrast wordmark set behind the device. Frames, cards and app UI are built in HTML; photoreal parts such as a real hand or lifestyle photography inside the screen are generated and composited in.

06

Logo and layout

Lowercase wordmark, near-black, tilde swoosh between wa and sal. 150px wide top-left on content slides, 216px on covers. Never recolor, never stretch.
Slide patterns Cover: gradient headline left, device visual right. Feature: headline plus eyebrow left, stacked glass cards right. Hub: orb centered, labeled cards around it. Divider: one large gradient phrase. Close: centered gradient line.
Spacing Slide padding 76-100px, card padding 32-46px, card gaps 20-34px. Content fills roughly the top three quarters, the base stays open as margin.
