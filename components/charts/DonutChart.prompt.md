Part to whole of one total. Segments are labeled beside the ring in their own accent, so no legend key is needed.

```jsx
<DonutChart
  segments={[{ label: "Mobile app", value: 62 }, { label: "Web", value: 26 }, { label: "Other", value: 12 }]}
  centerValue="62%" centerLabel="mobile" />
```

Keep it to three or four segments. More than that, use RankedBars.
